About the Columbia Math Circle

The Columbia Math Circle is an enrichment activity for middle and high school students who enjoy mathematics and would like to see more of it than is usually possible in a regular school course.

At a Math Circle, students work on interesting, open-ended problems and mathematical ideas. The emphasis is not on racing through material or memorizing techniques, but on experimenting, looking for patterns, making conjectures, explaining ideas, and learning how mathematicians think about unfamiliar problems. Topics may come from number theory, geometry, combinatorics, probability, logic, games, or other areas of mathematics.

No special preparation is required. Students should come ready to think, ask questions, try ideas, and discuss mathematics with other students and teachers. There is no cost to attend.

For examples of similar programs, see MathCircles.org, the Emory Math Circle, or the Julia Robinson Mathematics Festival.
